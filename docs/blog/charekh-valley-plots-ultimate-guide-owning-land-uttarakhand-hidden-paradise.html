Ask ten city families what they miss most, and many will say the same thing: quiet, clean air and a view that does not end at the next building. That is why land in the Uttarakhand hills has become one of the most talked-about purchases for people who want a second home, or a calm place to retire.

But buying land far from home can feel risky. Where is the road? Who looks after the plot when you are away? Are the papers clean? This guide walks you through those questions for plots in Charekh Valley, so you can decide with confidence.

A good hill plot is not only about the view. It is about the road, the papers and the people who look after it.

What makes Charekh Valley a hidden paradise

  • Pine forests and open hill views all around
  • Terraced plots planned along the natural slope
  • Located beside Katyuri Resort, so the area stays active
  • Paved internal roads and a gated entrance
  • Water and power provisions planned for each plot

Unlike a loose piece of farmland, Charekh Valley is a planned plotted community. You can read more on the project overview or find the route on the location page.

Who buys plots here?

People come to the valley for different reasons. See which of these sounds like you:

The weekend family

Wants a cottage to escape to a few times a year.

Good fit: a plot that is easy to reach from the main road, with a clear view.

The retiree

Dreams of a slower life surrounded by trees.

Good fit: a gentler slope, close to roads, utilities and the resort’s services.

The patient investor

Wants to hold scarce hill land for the long term.

Good fit: a plot with clean papers and strong road access. Ask about leasing too.

Your buying roadmap

Follow these five steps in order and you will avoid most first-time mistakes.

  1. Visit the site

    Walk the land, see the access road and look at the neighbouring resort. Photos never show slope or distance honestly. Browse the gallery first, then plan a visit.

  2. Study the master plan

    Find where each plot sits, how wide the roads are and which direction it faces. Our master plan page shows the layout.

  3. Verify the documents

    Ask for the title papers and have your own lawyer read them. Do this before you pay any booking amount.

  4. Confirm price and payment terms

    Check the rate for your plot and get the payment schedule in writing. See the pricing page for guidance.

  5. Register and plan your next move

    Complete the registry, then decide whether to build now, hold, or explore leasing.

Checklist before you pay

  • Title papers checked by your own lawyer
  • Plot boundaries marked clearly on the ground
  • A usable road reaches the plot, in every season
  • Written plan for water and electricity
  • Land-use details confirmed in writing
  • Payment schedule and receipts in your name
  • Current Uttarakhand rules on buying land checked
Good to know: Land-buying rules in Uttarakhand, especially for people from outside the state, have been changing. Always confirm the latest rules with a local lawyer before you book.

Choosing the right plot

Pick a plot that…

  • Opens toward the valley or the sunrise
  • Sits near a paved internal road
  • Has enough flat area for your house design
  • Matches the budget you set before visiting

Think twice if…

  • You have only seen it in photos
  • The seller will not share papers in writing
  • The slope is too steep for your plans
  • You are being rushed to pay today

Common questions

Can I buy a plot in Charekh Valley to build a holiday home?

Yes. Many buyers choose plots in Charekh Valley to build a weekend or retirement home. Ask our team for the plot dimensions, layout and construction guidelines before you decide.

Do I need to visit the site before booking a plot?

We strongly recommend it. A site visit lets you see the plot boundaries, the access road, the views and the neighbouring Katyuri Resort in person.

Can I lease my plot to the resort?

Leasing options can be discussed with our advisory team. Whatever is agreed, ask for the terms in writing before you book.
