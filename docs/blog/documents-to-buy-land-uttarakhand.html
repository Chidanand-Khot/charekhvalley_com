Essential Buyer Checklist for Mountain Plots

If you are planning to purchase land in the hills, make sure you verify these essential factors before signing the deed:

  1. Clear Legal Titles: Ensure land records are transparent and fully compliant with local state regulations.
  2. Road Connectivity: Verify that all-weather roads connect directly to the property boundary.
  3. Construction Guidelines: Understand local building bylaws, height restrictions, and eco-guidelines.
  4. Water & Power Source: Confirm reliable water supply lines and electrical infrastructure.
