What is Charekh Valley?

Charekh Valley is a plotted development in Uttarakhand, sitting right beside the established Katyuri Resort. Instead of an empty hillside, owners get a neighbour that is already operating, with hospitality, upkeep and a destination that travellers know.

The idea is simple. You buy a plot, and you decide what happens next. Some owners will build a private holiday home. Some will hold the land as a long-term asset. Others will look at leasing it to the resort. You can read the short version on the project overview page or go back to the Charekh Valley home page.

Why choose land in the Uttarakhand hills?

More families now want a second place away from the city: somewhere with clean air, forest, cooler weather and silence. Uttarakhand is a natural choice for many buyers, because the hills can be reached for a weekend or a long holiday, and the setting is very different from daily city life.

  • Clean mountain air: a real break from city pollution and noise.
  • Natural beauty: pine forests, mist and valley views.
  • Limited land: good hill land is scarce, which is why careful selection matters.
  • Tourism demand: hill destinations keep attracting visitors, which supports resort activity.

Location and surroundings

Charekh Valley is in the Uttarakhand hills, right beside Katyuri Resort. The surroundings are pine forest, open valley and quiet hill roads. Being next to an operating resort also makes the area easier to visit and easier to look after from a distance.

Who is Charekh Valley for?

  • Families who want a quiet second home in the hills.
  • Holiday home buyers who want to design their own cottage or villa.
  • Long-term land buyers who prefer a planned project beside an established resort.
  • Owners who want flexibility to build now, later, or lease.
Good to know: Hill land needs more care than city property. Slopes, access and paperwork matter. Take your time and ask questions.

Buyer's checklist before you pay

  1. Visit the site and the neighbourhood.
  2. Ask for the master plan and the exact plot dimensions in writing.
  3. Have a lawyer check ownership, land-use status and approvals.
  4. Confirm road, water and electricity arrangements.
  5. Understand maintenance costs and construction rules.
  6. Read every agreement fully before signing.
  7. Compare at least three projects before you decide.

Pricing and how to enquire

Plot prices depend on size, position and view, so we share the current price list directly. You can read the pricing page, then send an enquiry from the contact page. Before you enquire, note down your budget, the plot size you prefer, and whether you plan to build, hold or lease.

Planning your site visit

  1. Contact us and choose a date.
  2. Check the weather. Hill roads can be slow in rain and mist.
  3. Walk the plots and look at the slope, road and view.
  4. Ask for documents and the master plan.
  5. Visit the resort and see the surroundings.
  6. Take time to decide. A good plot will still be worth it after a few days of thought.
