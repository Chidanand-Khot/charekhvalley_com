Why Delhi NCR Residents Desperately Need a Mountain Weekend Home

Living in the National Capital Region offers unmatched career and business opportunities, but it comes with a severe toll on mental and physical well-being. Extreme summer temperatures touching 45°C, hazardous winter AQI levels, and relentless traffic noise make regular decompression essential.

Standard weekend trips to crowded hill stations like Nainital or Mussoorie often result in bumper-to-bumper traffic jams that defeat the very purpose of relaxation.
