The Nearest Railway Station: Kotdwara (KTW)

For travelers arriving by train, Kotdwara Railway Station (station code: KTW) serves as the primary railhead and closest railway station to Charekh Valley. Kotdwar is well-connected to major northern cities like Delhi via daily direct express and mail trains.

  • Train Route from Delhi: Popular options include the Garhwal Express and Mussoorie Express running overnight or morning services from Delhi to Kotdwar.
  • Onward Journey to Charekh Valley: Upon arriving at Kotdwara station, local taxicabs, private cars, or shared jeeps are readily available outside the platform to complete the remaining 23–25 km mountain drive, taking roughly 54 minutes to 1 hour.

The Nearest Airport: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun

If you are flying in from Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, or other major Indian metropolitan centers, Jolly Grant Airport (DED) near Dehradun is the closest airport facility.

Located approximately 145 to 155 km from the region, the airport offers regular direct flights operated by major domestic carriers. From the airport terminal, visitors can hire a pre-booked cab or rental vehicle to enjoy a breathtaking 4.5-hour scenic drive through Uttarakhand's foothills leading directly to Kotdwar and Charekh Valley.

Traveling by Road from Delhi & Major Hubs

Many homeowners prefer self-driving from Delhi, NCR, or neighboring states due to the excellent highway infrastructure. The classic route covers approximately 250 km and takes around 6 to 7 hours:

  1. Route Path: Delhi ➔ Ghaziabad ➔ Meerut ➔ Khatauli ➔ Bijnor ➔ Najibabad ➔ Kotdwar ➔ Charekh Valley.
  2. The Ascent: After crossing Kotdwar city and passing the Siddhbali Temple bridge, the scenic mountain road winds upward through serene pine forests directly into the valley.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the nearest railway station to Charekh Valley?

The nearest railway station is Kotdwara Railway Station (KTW), located approximately 23 to 25 kilometers away, offering smooth taxi and bus connectivity straight up to the valley.

Which airport is closest to Charekh Valley in Uttarakhand?

Jolly Grant Airport (DED) near Dehradun is the nearest airport, situated roughly 145 to 155 km away, providing daily flights from major Indian metropolitan hubs.

How long does it take to reach Charekh Valley from Kotdwar?

The scenic drive from Kotdwar city or station to Charekh Valley takes approximately 54 minutes to 1 hour through picturesque Garhwal hill roads.
