1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Charekh Valley (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your information when you visit www.charekhvalley.com or send us an enquiry. By using the website, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address and any message you send through the enquiry form or when you contact us.
  • Technical details: your device type, browser, approximate location, pages visited and how you found the website.
  • Cookies: small files that help the website work and help us understand how it is used.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and share the price list, plot details and site visit options.
  • To call, message (including WhatsApp) or email you about Charekh Valley.
  • To improve the website and our services.
  • To keep the website safe and to meet legal requirements.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Our team and authorised sales or property advisors who help with your enquiry.
  • Service providers who help run the website, forms, messaging or analytics, and who must protect your data.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to do so.

5. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools to see how many people visit, which pages are useful and how to make the website better. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the website may not work well without them.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, to meet legal needs or for genuine business reasons. After that we delete them or make them anonymous.

7. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no website or internet transfer is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop calling, messaging or emailing you.

To do any of these, please contact us.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you think a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as maps or social media. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Please check it now and then. Our Terms and Conditions also apply to your use of the website.

12. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your information, please contact us, write to email or call phone.
